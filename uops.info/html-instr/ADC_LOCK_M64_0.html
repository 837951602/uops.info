Summary: "Add With Carry"
Reference: https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/adc
Extension: BASE
Category: BINARY
ISA-Set: I86

iform: ADC_LOCK_MEMv_IMMb
iclass: ADC_LOCK

Operands

  • Operand 2 (r): 8-bit immediate
