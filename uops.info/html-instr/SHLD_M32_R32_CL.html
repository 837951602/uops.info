SHLD (M32, R32, CL)

Summary: "Double Precision Shift Left"
Reference: https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/shld
Extension: BASE
Category: SHIFT
ISA-Set: I386
CPL: 3
iform: SHLD_MEMv_GPRv_CL
iclass: SHLD
ASM: SHLD

Operands

  • Operand 1 (r/w): Memory
  • Operand 2 (r): Register (EAX, ECX, EDX, EBX, ESP, EBP, ESI, EDI, R8D, R9D, R10D, R11D, R12D, R13D, R14D, R15D)
  • Operand 3 (r): Register (CL)
  • Operand 4 (w, suppressed): Flags (AF: undef, CF: cw, OF: undef, PF: cw, SF: cw, ZF: cw)
