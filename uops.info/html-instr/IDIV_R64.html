IDIV (R64)

Summary: "Signed Divide"
Reference: https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/idiv
Extension: BASE
Category: BINARY
ISA-Set: I86
CPL: 3
iform: IDIV_GPRv
iclass: IDIV
ASM: IDIV

Operands

  • Operand 1 (r): Register (RAX, RCX, RDX, RBX, RSP, RBP, RSI, RDI, R8, R9, R10, R11, R12, R13, R14, R15)
  • Operand 2 (r/w, suppressed): Register (RAX)
  • Operand 3 (r/w, suppressed): Register (RDX)
  • Operand 4 (w, suppressed): Flags (AF: undef, CF: undef, OF: undef, PF: undef, SF: undef, ZF: undef)
