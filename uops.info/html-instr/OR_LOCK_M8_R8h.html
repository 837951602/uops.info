OR_LOCK (M8, R8h)

Summary: "Logical Inclusive OR"
Reference: https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/or
Extension: BASE
Category: LOGICAL
ISA-Set: I86
CPL: 3
iform: OR_LOCK_MEMb_GPR8
iclass: OR_LOCK
ASM: LOCK OR

Operands

  • Operand 1 (r/w): Memory
  • Operand 2 (r): Register (AH, CH, DH, BH)
  • Operand 3 (w, suppressed): Flags (AF: undef, CF: w, OF: w, PF: w, SF: w, ZF: w)
