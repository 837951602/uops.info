IDIV (R16)

Summary: "Signed Divide"
Reference: https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/idiv
Extension: BASE
Category: BINARY
ISA-Set: I86
CPL: 3
iform: IDIV_GPRv
iclass: IDIV
ASM: IDIV

Operands

  • Operand 1 (r): Register (AX, CX, DX, BX, SP, BP, SI, DI, R8W, R9W, R10W, R11W, R12W, R13W, R14W, R15W)
  • Operand 2 (r/w, suppressed): Register (AX)
  • Operand 3 (r/w, suppressed): Register (DX)
  • Operand 4 (w, suppressed): Flags (AF: undef, CF: undef, OF: undef, PF: undef, SF: undef, ZF: undef)
