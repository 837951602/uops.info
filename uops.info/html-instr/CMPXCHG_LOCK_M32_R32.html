Summary: "Compare and Exchange"
Reference: https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/cmpxchg
Extension: BASE
Category: SEMAPHORE
ISA-Set: I486REAL
CPL: 3
iform: CMPXCHG_LOCK_MEMv_GPRv
iclass: CMPXCHG_LOCK
ASM: LOCK CMPXCHG

Operands
