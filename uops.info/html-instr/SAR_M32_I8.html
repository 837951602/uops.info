SAR (M32, I8)

Summary: "Shift"
Reference: https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/sal:sar:shl:shr
Extension: BASE
Category: SHIFT
ISA-Set: I186
CPL: 3
iform: SAR_MEMv_IMMb
iclass: SAR
ASM: SAR

Operands

  • Operand 1 (r/w): Memory
  • Operand 2 (r): 8-bit immediate
  • Operand 3 (w, suppressed): Flags (AF: undef, CF: w, OF: undef, PF: w, SF: w, ZF: w)
