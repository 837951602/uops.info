IMUL (R16, R16, I16)

Summary: "Signed Multiply"
Reference: https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/imul
Extension: BASE
Category: BINARY
ISA-Set: I186
CPL: 3
iform: IMUL_GPRv_GPRv_IMMz
iclass: IMUL
ASM: IMUL

Operands

  • Operand 1 (w): Register (AX, CX, DX, BX, SP, BP, SI, DI, R8W, R9W, R10W, R11W, R12W, R13W, R14W, R15W)
  • Operand 2 (r): Register (AX, CX, DX, BX, SP, BP, SI, DI, R8W, R9W, R10W, R11W, R12W, R13W, R14W, R15W)
  • Operand 3 (r): 16-bit immediate
  • Operand 4 (w, suppressed): Flags (AF: undef, CF: w, OF: w, PF: undef, SF: undef, ZF: undef)
