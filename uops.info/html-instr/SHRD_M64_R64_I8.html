SHRD (M64, R64, I8)

Summary: "Double Precision Shift Right"
Reference: https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/shrd
Extension: BASE
Category: SHIFT
ISA-Set: I386
CPL: 3
iform: SHRD_MEMv_GPRv_IMMb
iclass: SHRD
ASM: SHRD

Operands

  • Operand 1 (r/w): Memory
  • Operand 2 (r): Register (RAX, RCX, RDX, RBX, RSP, RBP, RSI, RDI, R8, R9, R10, R11, R12, R13, R14, R15)
  • Operand 3 (r): 8-bit immediate
  • Operand 4 (w, suppressed): Flags (AF: undef, CF: w, OF: undef, PF: w, SF: w, ZF: w)
