Summary: "Move Quadword from XMM to MMX Technology Register"
Reference: https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/movdq2q

Category: DATAXFER
ISA-Set: SSE2
CPL: 3
iform: MOVDQ2Q_MMXq_XMMq
