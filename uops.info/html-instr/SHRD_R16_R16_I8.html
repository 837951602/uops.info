SHRD (R16, R16, I8)

Summary: "Double Precision Shift Right"
Reference: https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/shrd
Extension: BASE
Category: SHIFT
ISA-Set: I386
CPL: 3
iform: SHRD_GPRv_GPRv_IMMb
iclass: SHRD
ASM: SHRD

Operands

  • Operand 1 (r/w): Register (AX, CX, DX, BX, SP, BP, SI, DI, R8W, R9W, R10W, R11W, R12W, R13W, R14W, R15W)
  • Operand 2 (r): Register (AX, CX, DX, BX, SP, BP, SI, DI, R8W, R9W, R10W, R11W, R12W, R13W, R14W, R15W)
  • Operand 3 (r): 8-bit immediate
  • Operand 4 (w, suppressed): Flags (AF: undef, CF: w, OF: undef, PF: w, SF: w, ZF: w)
