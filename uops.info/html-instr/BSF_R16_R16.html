BSF (R16, R16)

Summary: "Bit Scan Forward"
Reference: https://www.felixcloutier.com/x86/bsf
Extension: BASE
Category: BITBYTE
ISA-Set: I386
CPL: 3
iform: BSF_GPRv_GPRv
iclass: BSF
ASM: BSF

Operands

  • Operand 1 (w): Register (AX, CX, DX, BX, SP, BP, SI, DI, R8W, R9W, R10W, R11W, R12W, R13W, R14W, R15W)
  • Operand 2 (r): Register (AX, CX, DX, BX, SP, BP, SI, DI, R8W, R9W, R10W, R11W, R12W, R13W, R14W, R15W)
  • Operand 3 (w, suppressed): Flags (AF: undef, CF: undef, OF: undef, PF: undef, SF: undef, ZF: w)
