Summary: "Unsigned Multiply Without Affecting Flags"

Extension: BMI2
Category: BMI2
ISA-Set: BMI2
CPL: 3
iform: MULX_GPR32d_GPR32d_MEMd
iclass: MULX
ASM: MULX
